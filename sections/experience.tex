\subentry{Representational drift and neural decoding (with Kaushik Lakshminarasimhan)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Developed an efficient-coding account of representational drift; first-author paper under review at \textbf{ICLR 2027}.
  \item Quantified drift in the longitudinal THINGS-EEG2 dataset and showed that its geometry relative to the decoding axis determines whether decoding accuracy degrades, improves, or holds, reconciling prior reports of degradation and no effect (\textbf{CCN 2026}).

\subentry{Brain-aligned vision models, ReAlnet (with Zitong Lu and Julie Golomb, Ohio State University)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Co-designed a multi-layer image-to-brain encoding framework that trains CORnet-S jointly on ImageNet classification and EEG generation (MSE + contrastive loss), yielding \textbf{10 individualized models} aligned to single subjects' EEG.
  \item ReAlnets beat CORnet in EEG representational similarity across all four layers (\textbf{+3\% average, up to 40\% relative at peak}); gains transfer to unseen fMRI data and to human behavior on Brain-Score, and the method extends to ResNet-18.
  \item Built the Brain-Score behavioral evaluation and RSA analysis pipelines; ran control experiments (loss ablations, unpaired and scrambled EEG) isolating the contribution of neural alignment from visual diet. \textbf{3 publications} (\venue{Communications Biology} 2026, \venue{Cognitive Neurodynamics} 2025, ICLR 2024 workshop).
\end{itemize}
\subentry{Computational modeling and biomarker discovery in Alzheimer's disease}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Predicted conversion from mild cognitive impairment (MCI) to Alzheimer's disease \textbf{4 years before clinical diagnosis with 80\% specificity}, using computational biomarkers from individualized brain-network models and multivariate discriminant analysis.
  \item Built the models in The Virtual Brain (TVB) from structural MRI/DTI data and applied biophysical techniques (mean-field dynamics, criticality analysis) to simulate large-scale brain dynamics in MCI and AD.
  \item Managed clinical data from hundreds of patients across MRI, cognitive assessments, and behavioral measures.
\end{itemize}
