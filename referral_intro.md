# Yile (Wayne) Wang: third-person introduction for referrals

Use for Google referral forms or messages to hiring teams.
Application: Student Researcher, PhD, Winter-Summer 2027.


Yile (Wayne) Wang is a Ph.D. candidate in Cognition and Neuroscience at the University of Texas at Dallas, with an expected graduation in August 2027. His research builds neural networks with biological learning rules to explain representational drift and the stability-plasticity problem in neural circuits, leading toward continual learning.

He studies which learning rules produce drift, with the aim of turning biological learning rules into the next generation of machine learning. His first-author paper on an efficient-coding account of representational drift is under review at ICLR 2027. His dissertation asks how brain circuits extract invariants from drifting activity and weights, the question at the heart of continual learning. He also co-developed ReAlnet, a framework that aligns CNN vision models with individual human EEG signals, published in *Communications Biology* (2026).

Outside the lab, he created and maintains LLM-for-Zotero, an open-source AI research agent with over 3,100+ GitHub stars and 650,000+ downloads. He designed its adaptive agent loop, which plans, executes tools, and continues based on observed progress, and its retrieval over each user's own library, making it a personalized agent for academic research. For him, AI for science has two layers: building useful AI tools for scientists, and building brain-like AI systems that advance science itself.

He is interested in projects on continual learning, neuroscience-inspired learning, or agents for science at Google Research or DeepMind, and is available from January to August 2027.
